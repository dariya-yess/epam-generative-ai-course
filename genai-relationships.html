Methodology
Explicit counts are case-insensitive regex matches of each term/abbreviation in research-report.md (with word boundaries where appropriate).
Implicit counts are evidence-based at sentence level: a sentence counts only if it clearly describes the concept using term-specific patterns (generic words like workflow/context/assistant/review/API are not counted). Sentences that already contain an explicit mention for that same term are excluded. Duplicate sentences per term are counted once.
Note: Implicit rules are conservative and may undercount concepts that are present but not clearly described in a sentence without the term itself.
